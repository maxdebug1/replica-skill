# Vlog channel

Daily life, travel, family, moving, building a business in public. The viewer watches for the person and the story.

## Formats and lengths

| format | length | role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| story vlog | 10 to 25 min | Hub; the core |
| milestone or event | 15 to 30 min | Hero; moves, trips, launches |
| Shorts | 15 to 45 s | a moment with a feeling |
| community posts and live | ongoing | closeness |

Lengths are starting points [rule of thumb]. The right length is the length
the idea needs; check average percentage viewed by length band on the
channel itself.

## Cadence and pillars

- Cadence: regular and predictable; viewers build a habit around it.
- Pillars: a recurring life thread (the build, the trip, the journey), plus occasional topic-led episodes for discovery.

## Packaging

- Titles: the story's question or turning point, not the date ("We sold the house"). Avoid "Vlog #47".
- Thumbnails: a face and a moment with emotion, the setting recognisable.

## Retention

- Every episode needs a story spine: a goal, an obstacle, a change. Footage without a question is a home movie.
- Open on the most dramatic moment, then rewind.
- Cut harder than feels natural; long scenic stretches lose people.

## Monetization fit

Sponsorships that fit the life shown, fan funding and memberships, affiliate for gear, merchandise. Ads often moderate.

See `references/monetization-guide.md` for requirements.

## Pitfalls

- Privacy: of children, partners, addresses and routines. Decide limits early.
- Burnout from filming everything; batch and keep boundaries.
- Hard to be discovered by strangers; use topic-led episodes and Shorts.

## Metrics to watch first

Returning viewers, subscribers per 1,000 views, average view duration, comments per view.
