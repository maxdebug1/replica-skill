# Commentary channel

Commentary, essays, analysis and reaction to news, culture, internet events, film and games.

## Formats and lengths

| format | length | role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| commentary on a current event | 8 to 20 min | Browse; timely |
| video essay | 20 to 60 min | Hero; evergreen depth |
| reaction with analysis | 10 to 20 min | Hub |
| Shorts | 30 to 60 s | the sharpest take |

Lengths are starting points [rule of thumb]. The right length is the length
the idea needs; check average percentage viewed by length band on the
channel itself.

## Cadence and pillars

- Cadence: speed matters for news; depth matters for essays. Mix both.
- Pillars: the beat (topic area) the channel owns, a recurring format, occasional big essays.

## Packaging

- Titles: the take or the question, not just the event name ("Why the X launch failed"). Avoid misleading claims about people.
- Thumbnails: the subject of the commentary plus the creator's reaction; respect others' rights over images.

## Retention

- State the take in the first 30 seconds; the viewer came for a point of view.
- Structure as an argument: claim, evidence, counter-argument, conclusion.
- Sources on screen and in the description.

## Monetization fit

Ads, sponsorships, memberships and Patreon (for long essays), merchandise. Some topics are limited by advertiser-friendly guidelines.

See `references/monetization-guide.md` for requirements.

## Pitfalls

- Fair use and copyright: use only what the commentary needs, transform it, and add real analysis; it is a legal judgment, not a guarantee.
- Reused content policy: reaction without meaningful commentary may not be monetizable.
- Defamation and harassment policies: criticise ideas and public actions with evidence, never target private individuals.
- Burnout from the news cycle.

## Metrics to watch first

Velocity in the first 48 hours, Browse share, average view duration, returning viewers.
