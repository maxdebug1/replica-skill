# Education channel

Explains ideas: science, history, economics, language, how things work. The viewer wants to understand, not to do a task right now.

## Formats and lengths

| format | length | role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| explainer | 10 to 25 min | Hub and Hero; the core |
| short explainer | 4 to 8 min | Help; one question, answered |
| Shorts | 30 to 60 s | one surprising fact with a turn; discovery |
| series or course playlist | 5 to 10 parts | session depth, Suggested |

Lengths are starting points [rule of thumb]. The right length is the length
the idea needs; check average percentage viewed by length band on the
channel itself.

## Cadence and pillars

- Cadence: one long video every one to two weeks is normal for research-heavy work; quality and accuracy outrank volume.
- Pillars: a recurring question type ("why does X"), a series on one subject, and occasional big Hero explainers.

## Packaging

- Titles: the question or the surprising claim ("Why ships don't tip over"); curiosity over keywords for Browse, the plain question for search.
- Thumbnails: one striking visual of the concept, a diagram or object, little text. Faces optional.

## Retention

- Hook with the question and why the obvious answer is wrong.
- Visuals carry the explanation: diagrams, animation, demonstrations. Plan `[GFX]` beats every 20 to 40 s.
- Build in layers: simple model, the complication, the better model.

## Monetization fit

Ads (education usually attracts reasonable advertiser demand), sponsorships from learning tools and apps, courses and books, memberships for bonus content.

See `references/monetization-guide.md` for requirements.

## Pitfalls

- Accuracy: cite sources in the description; errors in the comments cost trust fast. Pin corrections.
- Over-long setup before the interesting part.
- Topics so broad they compete with the biggest channels; find an angle only this creator has.

## Metrics to watch first

Average percentage viewed within length bands, returning viewers, Suggested share from other education channels.
