# Personal brand channel

A founder, professional, artist or expert whose channel serves their wider career: audience, clients, a business, a book.

## Formats and lengths

| format | length | role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| opinion or lessons | 8 to 15 min | Hub; voice and point of view |
| how-I or behind the scenes | 10 to 20 min | proof |
| interviews and conversations | 20 to 60 min | network, Suggested |
| Shorts | 30 to 60 s | clips of the strongest opinions |

Lengths are starting points [rule of thumb]. The right length is the length
the idea needs; check average percentage viewed by length band on the
channel itself.

## Cadence and pillars

- Cadence: one a week is sustainable alongside a job; batch filming.
- Pillars: the expertise, the story (journey, lessons), the network (guests).

## Packaging

- Titles: lessons and outcomes from first-hand experience ("What I learned hiring 50 people"). The person's name is not the hook until it is famous.
- Thumbnails: the face consistently, with a short statement or the outcome.

## Retention

- Story beats with a lesson; specific numbers and real examples beat generic advice.
- The creator's opinion stated plainly; agreeable content is forgettable.

## Monetization fit

The business it supports (clients, products, hiring), speaking, books, sponsorships. Ads are a bonus.

See `references/monetization-guide.md` for requirements.

## Pitfalls

- Measuring by views alone when the goal is leads or opportunities; track those.
- Corporate polish that hides the person.
- Confidential information about employers or clients.

## Metrics to watch first

Enquiries, newsletter sign-ups from the channel, subscribers per 1,000 views, returning viewers.
