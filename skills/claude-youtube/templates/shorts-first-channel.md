# Shorts-first channel

A channel whose main output is Shorts: comedy, tips, facts, satisfying processes, quick reviews, clips of longer work.

## Formats and lengths

| format | length | role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short | 15 to 60 s (up to 3 min allowed) | the core |
| series of Shorts | recurring | recognisable formats |
| long form (optional) | 8 to 20 min | depth, higher RPM |
| live | optional | fan funding |

Lengths are starting points [rule of thumb]. The right length is the length
the idea needs; check average percentage viewed by length band on the
channel itself.

## Cadence and pillars

- Cadence: daily or several a week is common; the feed rewards a steady supply of strong ideas, not volume of weak ones.
- Pillars: two or three repeatable Shorts formats with a recognisable opening.

## Packaging

- Titles: short, descriptive, searchable; they appear less than in long form.
- Thumbnails: matter less in the feed but still show on the channel page and in search; pick a clear frame.

## Retention

- The first second is the hook: motion, a face, a surprising line. No intro.
- One idea, a turn, and a loop back to the start.
- Captions on screen; safe zones clear (see `references/shorts-playbook.md`).

## Monetization fit

The Shorts revenue pool (low RPM per view), sponsorships for Shorts integrations, affiliate, products, and long-form for higher RPM. YPP via 10 million Shorts views in 90 days, or fan funding via 3 million.

See `references/monetization-guide.md` for requirements.

## Pitfalls

- Low revenue per view; plan other streams early.
- Audiences that do not follow to long form.
- Trend-chasing that leaves no identity; keep a recognisable series.
- Reused content (compilations of others' clips) is not monetizable.

## Metrics to watch first

Viewed vs swiped away, average percentage viewed, engaged views, subscribers per 1,000 views.
