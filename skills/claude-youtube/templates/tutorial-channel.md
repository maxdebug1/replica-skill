# Tutorial channel

Shows how to do a specific task: software, cooking, DIY, repairs, crafts, fitness. The viewer has a problem now and wants it solved.

## Formats and lengths

| format | length | role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| how-to | 5 to 15 min | Help; the core, search-driven |
| complete guide | 20 to 60 min | Hub; chaptered, bookmarked |
| tips roundup | 8 to 12 min | Browse; "10 things I wish I knew" |
| Shorts | 20 to 45 s | one tip or one fix |

Lengths are starting points [rule of thumb]. The right length is the length
the idea needs; check average percentage viewed by length band on the
channel itself.

## Cadence and pillars

- Cadence: one or two a week; a large back catalogue of evergreen answers compounds.
- Pillars: beginner path, specific fixes, tools and setup reviews.

## Packaging

- Titles: the task in the viewer's words, front-loaded ("How to fix a leaking tap in 5 minutes"). Search-first. Include the version or year where it matters.
- Thumbnails: the result or the problem, big and clear; the tool or software logo can help recognition. A face is optional.

## Retention

- Hook: show the finished result, then promise the steps. Skip the backstory.
- Chapters are essential; viewers jump to their step.
- Show, then tell. Zoom on the detail that matters. Keep the steps in order and say which step we are on.

## Monetization fit

Affiliate links for tools used, digital products (templates, courses), services, sponsorships from tools in the niche. Ad rates depend heavily on the subject (software and finance higher than crafts).

See `references/monetization-guide.md` for requirements.

## Pitfalls

- Outdated videos: update or pin a correction when software changes.
- Long intros before the steps; viewers leave for a faster video.
- Skipping the common mistake that made them search in the first place.

## Metrics to watch first

Search share, average view duration, returning viewers from playlists, comments asking follow-up questions (next videos).
