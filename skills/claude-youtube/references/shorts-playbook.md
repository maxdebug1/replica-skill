# Shorts playbook

How Shorts are distributed and what that means for making them. Evidence
levels as in `algorithm-guide.md`.

## Format [official]

- Vertical (9:16) or square video, up to 3 minutes.
- A Short can use sound from YouTube's library or from other Shorts and
  videos, within the remix rules; a Short with a claimed copyrighted track
  over one minute may be blocked.
- A Short can link to one of the creator's long videos, shown as a link in
  the Shorts player ("related video").
- Titles and descriptions still matter for search and context; hashtags are
  optional.

## How the feed works

- Viewers did not choose the Short; it appears in a swipe feed. YouTube has
  said the key signal is whether viewers choose to watch rather than swipe
  away [official].
- Studio shows "Viewed vs. swiped away" for each Short [official]. It is the
  closest thing Shorts have to CTR.
- Shorts are recommended in their own system. YouTube has said Shorts views
  do not hurt a channel's long-form performance [official].
- Shorts views and long-form views are counted differently for monetization
  and for YPP eligibility (see `monetization-guide.md`) [official].
- "Engaged views" in Studio excludes some quick swipes and replays and is
  closer to real interest than raw views [official].

## What tends to work [observed]

- **The first second carries the hook**: motion, a face mid-sentence, a
  surprising image or line. No logo, no intro.
- **One idea per Short.** A complete thought with a turn: setup, twist or
  reveal, end.
- **Loops.** An ending that flows back into the start increases replays and
  average percentage viewed, which can exceed 100 percent.
- **Text on screen and captions**: much of the feed is watched without sound.
- **Native making**: shot or cut for vertical. A horizontal video with
  bars above and below rarely holds attention.
- **Consistency of subject**: Shorts that build a recognisable series do
  better than one-offs [rule of thumb].
- **Length**: most successful Shorts sit well under a minute, but the right
  length is the length of the idea [rule of thumb].

## Safe zones [observed]

Keep text and faces out of the bottom roughly 20 percent (title, channel
name, audio label) and the right edge (like, comment, share buttons). Check
on a phone before publishing.

## Shorts and long form

- Shorts reach viewers who may never watch long form. Conversion from Shorts
  viewer to long-form viewer is often low; judge the strategy on the role
  chosen in `sub-skills/shorts.md`, not on that ratio alone [observed].
- Use the related-video link and a topic that is genuinely a slice of the
  long video when the role is discovery.
- Cutting Shorts from long videos is the cheapest supply; filming natively is
  usually better quality [rule of thumb].

## Monetization [official]

Shorts earn from a revenue-sharing pool funded by ads between Shorts. The
creator share is 45 percent of the revenue allocated to them after music
licensing costs. Shorts RPM is typically far lower than long-form RPM
[observed]; compare Shorts RPM only with Shorts RPM.

## Metrics to read

Views, engaged views, viewed vs swiped away, average view duration, average
percentage viewed, subscribers gained, and (for discovery role) clicks on
the related video. Compare Shorts with the channel's other Shorts.
