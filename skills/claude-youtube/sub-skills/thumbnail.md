# thumbnail: concepts, briefs and critiques

A thumbnail and title are one unit: the thumbnail makes the viewer stop, the
title tells them why to click. Designing one without the other wastes both.

Read: `references/thumbnail-ctr-guide.md`. The CTR ranges there are
context, not goals.

## Concepts for a new video

Inputs: the title (or 3 candidate titles), the video's single most visual
moment, the channel's existing thumbnail style, and who appears on camera.

Give three concepts that are genuinely different, not three crops of one
idea:

```
Concept {{A}}: {{one-line idea}}
Pairs with title: {{title}}
Focal point: {{the one thing the eye lands on}}
Subject: {{face and expression / object / before-after / result}}
Text: {{0 to 3 words, or none. Never the title repeated.}}
Composition: {{left/right split, rule of thirds, scale contrast}}
Colour: {{dominant colour and contrast against YouTube's light and dark UI}}
Curiosity gap: {{what it shows and what it deliberately does not}}
Shot list: {{what to photograph or capture from the edit}}
```

Then the brief for whoever makes it: 1280 x 720 (16:9), legible at the
smallest mobile size (check it at about 160 px wide), nothing important in
the bottom-right corner where the duration stamp sits.

## Critique of existing thumbnails

For each one (ask for images, or use the video URLs):

1. **Squint test.** At a small size, is there one clear focal point?
2. **Text.** Words count and readability. Does it add to the title or
   repeat it?
3. **Emotion or stakes.** Is there a reason to feel something?
4. **Contrast.** Against the feed, and against the channel's own last 10.
5. **Truth.** Does the video deliver what is pictured, early? A thumbnail
   that misleads wins the click and loses the session, and YouTube's
   policies on misleading metadata apply.

Tie each critique to data when there is any: impressions CTR vs the
channel's median for videos with similar impression counts `[Studio]`.

## Testing

Recommend YouTube's built-in Test and Compare in Studio (up to three
thumbnail variants; it picks by watch time share, not CTR alone) when the
channel has it. Change one variable between variants so the result teaches
something. Give it the full test period before judging.

## Policy

No sexualised imagery, graphic shock or fake UI elements meant to trick a
tap. Only use faces and logos you have the right to use.
