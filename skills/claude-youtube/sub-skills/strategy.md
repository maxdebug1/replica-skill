# strategy: positioning, pillars and a 90-day plan

Decides who the channel is for, what it makes, and how it will know it is
working. A strategy is a set of choices, so it must say what the channel
will not do.

Read: `references/algorithm-guide.md` (the audience-first model),
`references/repurposing-guide.md` (Hub, Hero, Help) and the channel's
template.

## Inputs

`youtube/context.md`, the audit if one exists, and three to five channels
the creator admires or competes with (run `competitor` on the closest one if
there is time).

## Steps

1. **The viewer.** One specific person: their situation, what they are trying
   to do, what they watch already. "Beginners" is not a viewer; "people who
   bought their first espresso machine and keep pulling sour shots" is.
2. **The promise.** One sentence the banner, trailer and every title can
   live under. Test: could a viewer predict the next video's topic?
3. **The edge.** Why this creator rather than the 50 channels already there:
   access, skill, personality, format, speed, depth. Be honest when there is
   no edge yet; the strategy then has to build one.
4. **Pillars.** Two to four repeatable content series, each mapped to Hub
   (regular, for subscribers), Help (search-driven, evergreen) or Hero
   (big, shareable, occasional). Each pillar gets a format, a length and an
   example title.
5. **Long vs Shorts.** What role Shorts play: discovery into long form, a
   standalone line, or none. See `sub-skills/shorts.md`.
6. **Cadence the creator can keep.** From their stated capacity. Consistency
   beats volume; a schedule they abandon in week 5 is worse than a slower
   one kept.
7. **Measures.** Two or three leading indicators per pillar (impressions
   CTR, average view percentage, subscribers per 1,000 views, returning
   viewers) checked at 28 days, and one lagging goal for 90 days.

## The 90-day plan

| weeks | focus | output | check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 2 | foundations: channel page, trailer, template, backlog of 10 ideas | ... | ... |
| 3 to 6 | publish pillar videos on cadence, one variable changed at a time | ... | 28-day read |
| 7 to 10 | double down on what over-performed, drop or change what did not | ... | ... |
| 11 to 13 | first Hero video or collaboration, review the plan | ... | 90-day review |

## Output: `youtube/strategy.md`

Viewer, promise, edge, pillars table, Shorts role, cadence, measures, the
90-day plan, and "we will not": the topics and formats deliberately left
out. Next: `ideate` to fill the pillars, then `calendar`.
