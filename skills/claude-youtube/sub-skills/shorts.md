# shorts: ideas, scripts and a Shorts strategy

Shorts are watched in a swipe feed where nobody chose your video, so the
first second does the work a thumbnail does for long form, and looping and
completion matter more than clicks.

Read: `references/shorts-playbook.md` in full, and the `shorts-first`
template if Shorts are the main format.

## Decide the role first

Ask which of these the Shorts are for, because the scripts differ:

1. **Discovery for long form.** Shorts that make a viewer want the full
   video. Link the related video (Studio lets you link one video from a
   Short) and keep the topic identical.
2. **A standalone line.** Shorts as the product; long form optional.
3. **Testing ideas.** Cheap tests of topics or angles before a long video.

Say plainly what the data says about Shorts viewers converting to long-form
viewers on this channel, if it is available `[Studio]`; it is often low,
and that is fine if the role is 2.

## A Short, scripted

```
Title (for search and the feed, not shown much): {{...}}
Length target: {{seconds}} (most land 20 to 45 s; up to 3 min is allowed)
0.0 s  Visual: {{the first frame, already in motion}}  Text on screen: {{0 to 6 words}}
0.0 s  Line: {{the hook, under 2 seconds spoken}}
...    Beats: {{one idea, delivered fast, a turn or reveal}}
end    Loop: {{how the last line or frame leads back into the first}}
Audio: {{original voice / licensed track from YouTube's library}}
```

Rules: vertical 9:16, captions burned in or on (much of the feed is
watched muted), safe zones clear of the UI on the right edge and bottom,
no intro, no "follow for part 2" unless part 2 exists.

## Ideas

Generate 10 per request from: moments in the creator's long videos with a
complete idea in under 45 seconds, questions from comments, a single tip,
a myth and its correction, a before and after, a reaction to a trend in the
niche. Mark each with its role (1, 2 or 3).

## Reading Shorts numbers

Use the Shorts-specific numbers in Studio: views, "engaged views",
viewed vs swiped away, average percentage viewed (can exceed 100 percent
with replays). Compare Shorts with Shorts only, never with long-form CTR or
watch time.

## Output

Role, 10 ideas, 3 full scripts, the posting cadence, saved to
`youtube/shorts.md`.
